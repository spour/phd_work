Figure X. Positional U1 duplex density and upstream 3' splice-site strength
discriminate real 3' ends from intragenic competitors.
A ridge (L2) logistic model with 26 features was fitted to distinguish each gene's
real 3' end from the competing intragenic cleavage sites called in the same gene,
over the 4,847 analysis-set genes (one real 3' end and at least one competitor per
gene, 48,394 candidate sites).
(A) Feature construction and model specification. Sequence is read in a
transcript-oriented window from -2,000 to +500 nt about the candidate cleavage site
(dashed line) and divided into 25 bins of 100 nt. Each bin contributes one feature,
the density of U1 duplex sites (predicted U1 snRNA:pre-mRNA duplex -dG >= 4
kcal/mol) per kb of scanned 9-mer positions (blue, 25 values). A single further
feature is the maximum MaxEntScan 3' splice-site score anywhere in [-2,000, -126]
(orange, 1 value), which is a scalar and carries no positional information. The 26
features are centred within gene and standardised before fitting, and the model is
evaluated by where it ranks the real 3' end among its own gene's candidates, never
by a pooled comparison across genes. (B) Ridge coefficients on the same positional
axis as A, so each bar sits above the sequence it weights; the shaded field is
upstream of the cleavage site. U1 duplex density carries negative weight throughout
the upstream region, deepening toward the site to a minimum of -0.23 at -150 nt:
denser predicted U1 binding just upstream argues against a position being the real
3' end. The upstream 3' splice-site scalar carries the single largest positive
weight, +0.30 (plotted to the right of the axis break because it is not positional):
a strong upstream acceptor argues for it. (C) Within-gene recall as a function of
rank threshold k, over all 4,847 genes, leave-one-chromosome-out, with the ridge
penalty retuned inside each fold on a 25% gene holdout of that fold's own training
genes, so both the fit and the hyperparameter are out of fold at every gene. Rank
is 1 + the number of competitors scoring at or above the real site, so ties count
against the model and recall@1 is the strict precision at 1. Blue, observed recall
with 95% CIs; grey dashed, exact chance for a random scorer, the mean of
min(k, n_i)/n_i over genes, which is curved rather than linear because genes differ
in the number of candidates they carry; the shaded band is the gap between them.
The model places the real 3' end first in 46.5% of genes (95% CI 45.1-47.9%,
chance 28.7%) and within the top three in 79.8% (78.7-80.9%, chance 69.3%).
(D) Distribution of model scores at real 3' ends and at competitors, from the same
leave-one-chromosome-out scoring as C, with each gene's mean score subtracted from
its own sites so that only within-gene contrasts are shown and every between-gene
difference is removed before plotting. Violins show the full distribution, boxes the
interquartile range and median (medians +0.33 and +0.00). The real 3' end outscores
the mean of its own gene's competitors in 69.9% of genes (95% CI 68.9-71.0%; median
per-gene difference +0.48, positive in 75% of genes; two-sided paired Wilcoxon
signed-rank on the per-gene differences, V = 9,486,959, n = 4,847, z = +37.1,
P < 2.2 x 10^-16). The win-rate and the signed-rank test are one result expressed
twice, being the same paired estimator, and panel C's recall@1 is recomputed from
panel D's scores as an equality check, so the two panels are one scoring of the
data. The separation is a shift of overlapping distributions: roughly a quarter of
genes run the other way, so this is a population-level property of where real 3'
ends sit and not a per-site classifier.

Figure X. Positional U1 duplex density and upstream 3' splice-site strength
discriminate real 3' ends from intragenic competitors. Ridge (L2) logistic model,
26 features, over the 4,847 analysis-set genes (one real 3' end and at least one
competitor each). (A) Feature construction: a transcript-oriented window from
-2,000 to +500 nt about the candidate site in 25 bins of 100 nt, each contributing
the density of U1 duplex sites (-dG >= 4 kcal/mol) per kb (blue, 25 values), plus
one scalar, the maximum MaxEntScan 3' splice-site score over [-2,000, -126]
(orange). Features are gene-centred and standardised; evaluation is the rank of the
real 3' end within its own gene. (B) Ridge coefficients on the same positional axis.
U1 duplex density is negatively weighted upstream, deepest at -150 nt (-0.23); the
upstream acceptor scalar carries the largest positive weight (+0.30) and is drawn
off the positional axis. (C) Within-gene recall against rank threshold k, all 4,847
genes, leave-one-chromosome-out with the penalty retuned in fold; ties count against
the model. Blue, observed with 95% CIs; grey dashed, exact chance (mean of
min(k, n_i)/n_i, curved because genes differ in candidate count). Recall@1 0.465
(0.451-0.479) against chance 0.287; recall@3 0.798 (0.787-0.809) against 0.693.
(D) Gene-centred scores from the same out-of-fold scoring (violins, boxes show
interquartile range and median; medians +0.33 and +0.00). The real 3' end beats the
mean of its own competitors in 69.9% of genes (68.9-71.0%; median difference +0.48,
positive in 75%; paired Wilcoxon V = 9,486,959, n = 4,847, z = +37.1,
P < 2.2 x 10^-16). Distributions overlap: this is a population-level shift, not a
per-site classifier.

     score_separation_sites.tsv              score_separation.py

           cnn_data.npz         sequence tokens; splice_model_26.py recomputes
                                u1_dens4 from them and asserts it reproduces
                                binned_profiles.npz exactly in float32
Scope      the 4,847 in_analysis_set genes (see the funnel figure)

Rebuild    python splice_model_26.py ; python recall_at_k.py ; python score_separation.py
           /cvmfs/.../r/4.5.0/bin/Rscript make_splice_model_final.R

Numbers deliberately NOT on this figure, so they are not confused with it
  splice_model_26_metrics.tsv holds the locked train/val fit scored once on the
  1,623 held-out test genes: within-gene win-rate 0.6993 [0.6823, 0.7174] and
  P@1 0.4720 [0.4473, 0.4966] (chance 0.2923, 14,198 peaks). Panels C and D instead
  report leave-one-chromosome-out over all 4,847 analysis-set genes; the two
  win-rate estimates agree to four decimals (0.69930 held out, 0.69932 LOCO) but
  they are different estimates of the same quantity and the script prints both so
  the panel cannot silently mix them. The 0.7473 whole-genome P@1 quoted elsewhere
  belongs to the earlier 51-feature model and is recomputed, not carried over.

Model history worth one sentence in the text
  This supersedes a 51-feature model that also carried a -dG >= 7 density track.
  Dropping that track changed the win-rate by +0.0036 [-0.0037, +0.0107], so the
  model halves with no measurable loss.
