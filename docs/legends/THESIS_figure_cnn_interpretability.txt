Figure: what the polyadenylation CNN reads, and what it does with a splice site
Methods, numbers, results, and the figure caption
==============================================================================

The figure has two panels answering two halves of one question. Panel A asks what the network's
first layer detects and where along the window each detector matters. Panel B asks what happens
causally when a splice site is written into a real genomic window.

The figure itself carries no explanatory text. Everything needed to read it is here.


================================================================================
PART I   METHODS
================================================================================

I.1  MODEL
----------
The reported model is the convolutional network trained by cnn_train.py over a 2,500 nt window,
positions -2,000 to +500 relative to the cleavage site, saved as cnn_model.pt at epoch 6. One
convolutional layer of 32 filters of width 19, then pooling to 100 nt bins, then a linear read-out.

Training: BCEWithLogitsLoss with pos_weight = n_negative / n_positive = 8.945, dropout 0.4, learning
rate 1e-3, weight decay 1e-6, batch 128, up to 60 epochs with patience 10.

Performance, held out: within-gene win rate 0.821, pooled AUROC 0.818, 0.847 leave-one-chromosome-out
over the analysis set.

I.2  DATA AND SPLIT
-------------------
48,394 peaks, split BY GENE so no gene contributes to more than one split.

  train  27,658     2,781 real 3' ends    24,877 competitors
  val     6,538       443 real 3' ends     6,095 competitors
  test   14,198     1,623 real 3' ends    12,575 competitors

Competitors are filtered baseline-model false positives: peaks the upstream model called that are
not real 3' ends. 24,877 + 6,095 + 12,575 = 43,547, the whole competitor set. Every number in the
figure uses the test split and uses all of it; nothing is subsampled. Windows containing an N token
are excluded, which removes none from the test split. The test split holds 1,623 genes with one
real 3' end each.

I.3  CALIBRATION, WHICH GOVERNS HOW PANEL B's AXIS MAY BE READ
--------------------------------------------------------------
Because training was class balanced by pos_weight = 8.945, the network's output is calibrated to a
50/50 prior rather than to the 11.4% prevalence of real 3' ends in the data. An untouched competitor
therefore has a median predicted probability of 0.6251, not something near 0.114.

Panel B's y axis is the raw sigmoid of the network's logit. It is a relative scale on which the two
splice sites move in opposite directions. It is NOT a posterior probability that a peak is a real
3' end, and 0.63 must not be read as "63% of these are real".

Ranking results are unaffected, since win rate and AUROC depend only on the order of scores.

cnn_model.pt also stores temperature = 1225.80 from a validation fit. Dividing logits by 1226 would
collapse every prediction onto 0.5, so that fit is degenerate. It is applied nowhere in this work.

I.4  PANEL A: FILTERS AND POSITIONAL CONTRIBUTION
-------------------------------------------------
LOGOS. Each row is one of the 32 first-layer filters, drawn as a position weight matrix over its 19
positions in bits. Rows are ordered by summed absolute positional contribution, most contributing at
the top. Each row is labelled with its identity, its information content in bits per column, and the
number of activating sites it has across the test set.

HEATMAP. The window is divided into 25 bins of 100 nt spanning -2,000 to +500. Each cell is that
filter's contribution to within-gene ranking in that bin, obtained by silencing the filter in that
band and re-scoring. Blue means silencing costs the real 3' end more than its competitors, so the
filter argues for the real end there; orange is the reverse. Contributions span -0.0721 to +0.0942.
Computed on 14,198 test peaks in 1,623 genes; the intact model's win rate is 0.8211.

One output bin sees 378 nt of input, so a 100 nt silencing band is narrower than what the model
reads at that position, and the heatmap's resolution should be read accordingly.

WHAT WAS REMOVED, AND WHY IT WAS REMOVED. Earlier versions labelled each filter with its nearest
match in a small hand-assembled reference list and a PWM correlation r against it, and annotated one
row as a polypyrimidine tract. Both were dropped from the figure because they are guesses presented
as measurements. The reference list is short, so every filter has a nearest entry whether or not it
detects anything; and a T-rich filter correlates with an acceptor reference because acceptors are
T-rich, not because the filter detects acceptors. The matches for the record:

  filter18   0.80 b/col      235 sites   no match above threshold, best r 0.614
  filter11   0.62 b/col    1,858 sites   no match above threshold, best r 0.626
  filter27   0.82 b/col   24,668 sites   acceptor_3ss_pyAG, r 0.943
  filter17   0.74 b/col       37 sites   no match above threshold, best r 0.466
  filter00   0.58 b/col    4,839 sites   acceptor_3ss_pyAG, r 0.747

filter27 is the one that used to be annotated "polypyrimidine tract". Its consensus is a pure T run
and it is by far the most used filter in the model, 24,668 activating sites against a median of
1,566. Calling it an acceptor detector is exactly the inference Panel B was built to test, and Panel
B does not support it, so the label had no business on the figure.

I.5  PANEL B: IMPLANTATION
--------------------------
ASSAY. A construct is written over the native bases at a FIXED position 230 nt upstream of the
cleavage site, in every window of the background, and the window is re-scored. The reported quantity
is the median predicted probability across windows. Tandem series repeat the unit n times, so the
insert grows with n and displaces that much native sequence.

No maximum over positions is taken. Earlier versions reported the best of 60 implantation positions;
this was dropped because the maximum is an upward-biased statistic taken separately for each
construct and each control, and it was shown to both inflate and mask effects.

BACKGROUND. All 12,575 held-out competitor windows. Real 3' ends were not used because they already
sit at a median probability of 0.952 and an activating construct has no room to rise there.
Competitors sit at 0.6251 and can move both ways, and "does adding a splice site to a competitor
make the model call it real" is the more direct question.

CONTROLS. Two families, both drawn rather than subtracted, because neither is flat everywhere and
choosing one after seeing the result would hide that.

  Mononucleotide shuffle, 20 draws. A permutation of the insert's own bases: same length, same base
  counts, different order. For TTTAACTTTCTTTTTTACAGACG the first three draws are
  ATCGATTTCATGTCTTCTAATTT, CATGTATTTACCTTTGTATTATC, CATCTTATTTTTCTACTAGTAGT.

  Derangement, all 9. Every base mapped to a different base under a permutation of {A,C,G,T} with no
  fixed point, applied to the whole insert. Under A>C C>A G>T T>G the same sequence becomes
  GGGCCAGGGAGGGGGGCACTCAT.

Each control sequence is implanted into the same 12,575 windows at the same position and scored
identically. The grey band is the range the two families span, each plus or minus the standard error
of its own mean over draws. The standard deviation ACROSS draws is larger, 0.09 for the shuffles and
0.17 for the derangements at four copies, which is why the mean of many draws is the control and a
single draw is not.

The dinucleotide shuffle used in earlier rounds was abandoned: for a poly-pyrimidine construct it
returned the motif itself in 10 of 10 draws.

I.6  DERIVING THE IMPLANTED SEQUENCES
--------------------------------------
Neither implanted sequence is a consensus string from a database.

5' SPLICE SITE, CAGGTGAGT, score5 10.86. The plain argmax of the U1 snRNA duplex free energy over
all 4^9 = 262,144 9-mers, decoded from the precomputed landscape and asserted in the pipeline rather
than trusted. It is NOT the textbook donor: the consensus carried in cnn_interp_lib is CAGGTAAGT and
the physical optimum differs at one position.

3' SPLICE SITE, TTTAACTTTCTTTTTTACAGACG, score3 10.28. Derived from THE MODEL. Greedy hill climbing
over single-base substitutions maximising the mean change in logit over 256 real 3' end windows: at
each step all 21 free positions by 3 alternatives are evaluated, the best accepted, stopping when
none improves. Constraints enforced at every step so the search never leaves the feasible set: AG
pinned at positions 19 and 20, MaxEntScan score3 at least 10, the 18 nt tract at least 80%
pyrimidine, and no G anywhere in the tract. Three restarts from random feasible seeds.

The constraints matter. Without the pyrimidine and no-G requirements the same search returns
ACGGTGTACTTTTGTTGCAGGTG, whose tract is 61% pyrimidine with 28% G and does not read as an acceptor.
Adding them costs 0.31 in logit and buys a sequence that does.

I.7  DERIVING THE TWO LOGOS BESIDE PANEL B
-------------------------------------------
One recipe, the measure swapped, which is the comparison the panel exists to make: keep every
sequence whose own measure lies within a threshold of that measure's optimum, weight each linearly
by closeness as weight = value - (optimum - threshold), count weighted bases by position, report
bits.

  5' SPLICE SITE. Exhaustive. All 262,144 9-mers are already scored, so the 115 within 2 kcal/mol of
  the optimum are kept. A physical cut rather than a rank cut, so 115 is a result and not a choice.
  GT was not pinned. Consensus CAGGTAAGG.

  3' SPLICE SITE. The 23 nt register has 4^21 feasible sequences, so the ensemble is SAMPLED by
  Metropolis-Hastings: target exp(delta / temperature) restricted to the same feasible set as the
  search in I.6, proposal one random feasible single-base substitution, 128 chains for 900 steps,
  first 200 discarded, temperature 0.0355 set from the seed's own delta. 668 distinct sequences
  within 0.272 of the best delta found, consensus TTTTTCTCTTTTTTTTACAGCGT, mean 1.25 bits per column.

Two methodological notes. Sampling temperature must scale with the height of the peak: a fixed 0.08
held the chains on the unconstrained acceptor at +1.85 but on the constrained one at +0.82 gave 0.70
acceptance, the chain mean fell from +0.80 to +0.10, and only 7 distinct near-optimal sequences were
recovered, which is one sequence drawn as a logo. And the weighting must match across logos: an
earlier version weighted the acceptor exponentially while the donor was linear, giving a
threshold-edge sequence 37% weight where the other gave it zero.

I.8  STATISTICS
---------------
Intervals on the median probability are 95% percentile bootstraps over windows, 2,000 resamples.
Permutation tests elsewhere in this work use 500 permutations of a sequence's own bases. No sklearn
or scipy; AUROC, logistic regression and Wilcoxon are implemented inline.


================================================================================
PART II   RESULTS
================================================================================

II.1  PANEL A
-------------
Positional contribution is concentrated, not diffuse. The five largest contributors by summed
absolute contribution are filters 18, 11, 27, 17 and 00, at 0.707, 0.669, 0.656, 0.639 and 0.584.
Contributions span -0.0721 to +0.0942 across 25 bins and 32 filters.

Information content is low throughout, 0.55 to 0.82 bits per column. These are compositional
detectors, not structured motifs, and that is the honest reading of the logo column.

Site counts vary by three orders of magnitude, from 37 for filter17 to 24,668 for filter27, so a
filter's contribution is not a function of how often it fires.

II.2  PANEL B, THE 3' SPLICE SITE
----------------------------------
Implanted into 12,575 competitor windows whose untouched median probability is 0.6251:

  copies   median p         95% CI            shuffles   derangements
    1       0.799      [0.792, 0.805]           0.642        0.596
    2       0.932      [0.930, 0.935]           0.676        0.579
    3       0.975      [0.975, 0.976]           0.689        0.560
    4       0.985      [0.985, 0.986]           0.706        0.538

One copy of a 23 nt acceptor moves the median competitor from 0.63 to 0.80; four copies to 0.985.
Both control families stay near baseline over the same range. The gain over the shuffles is +0.28 at
four copies and over the derangements +0.45.

The intervals are narrow, roughly 0.013 at one copy and 0.001 at four, because the sample is every
held-out competitor rather than a subsample. At four copies the interval is narrower than the
plotted point, which is why the values are printed on the panel.

II.3  PANEL B, THE 5' SPLICE SITE
----------------------------------
  copies   median p         95% CI            shuffles   derangements
    1       0.593      [0.584, 0.604]           0.620        0.626
    4       0.451      [0.439, 0.462]           0.593        0.624
    8       0.331      [0.320, 0.341]           0.564        0.610

The donor moves the same windows the other way, to 0.331 at eight copies. The derangement control is
markedly the flatter of the two here, holding within 0.015 of baseline across all eight doses while
the shuffle drifts to 0.564; for the acceptor the relationship is reversed. This is why both are
drawn.

II.4  THE COMPARISON THE PANEL MAKES
-------------------------------------
The two sites move in opposite directions by comparable amounts, +0.36 and -0.29 in probability,
with both control families near the untouched baseline throughout. A donor is read as evidence
AGAINST a real 3' end and more of it is worse. An acceptor is read as evidence for one, but only
when it is delivered as T rather than as C, which is the subject of the companion analysis.

II.5  HOW FAR THE DOSE AXIS CAN BE READ
----------------------------------------
Both series stop at an insert length near 100 nt, four acceptor copies at 92 nt and eight donor
copies at 72 nt, for reasons established by carrying the units to 24 copies.

The logit never saturates: the acceptor gains about +1.6 per copy all the way out, reaching +30.97
at 24 copies. What fails is interpretation. Median probability passes 0.999 at three copies, so the
scale is exhausted. The share of windows whose logit exceeds +16.60, the highest value the model
emits on any held-out sequence, crosses 5% at seven copies, 39.7% at eight and 100% at fourteen.
Separately, past about 46 nt every homopolymer insert lands between -0.4 and -2.0 regardless of base,
so long inserts are dominated by a generic length penalty. Both failure modes bite by about four
copies.
