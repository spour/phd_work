CAPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Figure X. Construction of the analysis set by successive filtering of candidate
peaks. Attrition funnel over the 70,723 CPA peaks called across 6,583 protein-coding
genes, one annotated ("canonical", real) 3' end per gene plus 64,140 model-predicted
competitor peaks. Each bar is left-aligned on a shared count axis and its length is
the number of peaks that survive that stage; the shaded notch between consecutive
bars is what the intervening step removed, annotated with its size and criterion.
Row labels give the surviving peak and gene counts. Filters act on the competitor
pool only. (i) Competitors whose nearest same-strand PolyASite 2.0 cluster or
PolyA_DB v4 site lies within 500 nt are removed as annotated alternative 3' ends
(-18,119). (ii) Competitors whose 500 nt strand-oriented upstream window overlaps a
GENCODE v49 last exon by at least 1 nt are removed as reference terminal-exon peaks
(-1,076); the two exclusions are applied as a single conjunction, so the 5,294-peak
overlap between them is attributed to the APA criterion, which is listed first.
(iii) A gene is retained only if exactly one canonical peak and at least one
competitor survive, which drops 1,660 genes and their canonical peaks (6,583 ->
4,923 genes). (iv) Competitors at or downstream of their gene's canonical peak in
transcript coordinates are removed (-1,398), and (v) the gene rule is re-applied,
costing a further 76 genes. The final bar is split by class: 4,847 real 3' ends
(blue) and 43,547 competitors (orange), 48,394 peaks in 4,847 genes. Two of the five
steps therefore remove canonical rather than competitor peaks, because a gene that
runs out of competitors leaves the set together with its canonical peak. Counts are
not transcribed from pipeline logs: trace_analysis_set.py reconstructs the mask from
the raw annotation caches (atlas_peak_cache.npz, polyadb_peak_cache.npz,
termexon_gc_cache.npz) and asserts that it is identical to corr_rowmask.npz,
upstream_rowmask.npz and the in_analysis_set column of cpa_peaks_final.parquet.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SHORT VARIANT (if the caption must fit under ~120 words)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Figure X. Construction of the analysis set by successive filtering of candidate
peaks. Bar length is the number of peaks surviving each stage (shared axis, left
aligned); the shaded notch between bars is what the step removed. Starting from
70,723 called peaks in 6,583 genes (one annotated 3' end per gene, 64,140
competitors), competitors are removed if APA-annotated (nearest same-strand
PolyASite 2.0 or PolyA_DB v4 site within 500 nt; -18,119) or if their 500 nt
upstream window overlaps a GENCODE v49 last exon (-1,076); genes are kept only with
one canonical and >= 1 competitor (-1,660 peaks); competitors at or downstream of
the canonical are removed (-1,398); the gene rule is re-applied (-76). Two steps
therefore remove canonical peaks, since a gene without competitors leaves with its
canonical. The analysis set is 48,394 peaks in 4,847 genes: 4,847 real 3' ends
(blue) and 43,547 competitors (orange).

  termexon_gc_cache.npz["te_window"]  GENCODE v49 last-exon overlap flag

  upstream_rowmask.npz["ROW"]         checked against (stages 4-6)
  cpa_peaks_final.parquet             checked against (in_analysis_set column)

Where the definition lives in the pipeline (two scripts, not one)
  task_rerank_corr.py        APA + terminal-exon exclusions, then the gene rule
  task_upstream_blocks.py    upstream-of-canonical rule, then the gene rule again
  task_build_final_parquet.py  copies the resulting mask into in_analysis_set
