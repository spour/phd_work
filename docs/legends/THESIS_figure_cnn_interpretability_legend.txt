Figure X. Sequence determinants learned by the polyadenylation CNN, and the
causal effect of implanting a splice site into a competitor window.
Both panels use the reported model (cnn_model.pt) on its held-out split, 14,198
peaks in 1,623 genes (1,623 real 3' ends and 12,575 competitors), at an intact
within-gene win-rate of 0.821.
(A) Every first-layer filter and where along the window it moves the within-gene
ranking. Left, the sequence logo of each of the 32 width-19 filters, estimated from
the sites activating it above 30% of that filter's maximum, labelled with its
information content per column and the number of activating sites. Right, the
signed contribution of that filter in each of 25 bands of 100 nt, obtained causally
by silencing one filter in one band at a time on the unchanged model:
contribution = (mean over real 3' ends of the intact minus occluded logit) - (the
same mean over competitors). Blue means the filter in that band pushes real 3' ends
above their own gene's competitors and orange means it argues the other way; the
scale is symmetric about zero. Rows are ordered by total absolute contribution, so
the filters that move the ranking most sit at the top, and each logo is drawn beside
its own row: the pairing is verified in code rather than assumed, by re-deriving the
per-column consensus from each matrix as drawn and asserting it against the
consensus recorded for that filter. Contribution concentrates in the last few
hundred nucleotides upstream of the cleavage site rather than being spread across
the window, and the largest single detector is a polypyrimidine filter (filter 27,
0.82 bits per column, 24,668 activating sites, consensus poly-T), which is both the
highest in information content and the most used. Filters are not annotated with
reference-motif matches: a T-rich filter correlates with an acceptor reference
because acceptors are T-rich, not because the filter detects acceptors, so only the
measured quantities (identity, information content, site count, positional
contribution) are shown.
(B) Implantation. Each construct is written into every held-out competitor window
at position -230 in tandem copies, and the window is re-scored. Points are the
median predicted probability over all 12,575 competitor windows with 95% percentile
bootstrap intervals (2,000 resamples over windows); the dashed line is the median
of the untouched competitor windows (0.63). The grey band is the control envelope,
spanned by two controls that are drawn rather than subtracted: 20 mononucleotide
shuffles of each construct, which hold its length and composition exactly and vary
only the order, and all 9 derangements of ACGT, which substitute every base and hold
nothing, each widened by its own standard error across draws. Probability is plotted
rather than the logit because the logit is unbounded and keeps climbing after the
prediction it encodes has stopped moving. Competitor windows are used because real
3' ends already sit at a median probability of 0.95 and an activating construct has
nowhere to rise. Left, a 3' splice site (TTTAACTTTCTTTTTTACAGACG, tract 83%
pyrimidine with no G, MaxEntScan score3 10.28): one copy raises the median from 0.63
to 0.799 (95% CI 0.792-0.805) and four copies to 0.985, while the controls stay
inside the band. Right, a 5' splice site (CAGGTGAGT, the strongest U1 duplex of all
262,144 9-mers, score5 10.86): one copy gives 0.593 (0.584-0.604) and eight copies
drive the median down to 0.331. The model therefore treats an upstream acceptor as
evidence for a real 3' end and an upstream donor as evidence against, and the
direction is causal, not correlational. Beside each part is the position weight
matrix of an ensemble built by one recipe, sampling sequences whose own measure lies
near that measure's optimum and weighting each by how close it is; the measures
differ, which is the comparison. For the acceptor the measure is THE MODEL, sampled
by Metropolis-Hastings over 23-mers with the AG pinned and score3 held at or above
10, so every sequence in the ensemble is still a splice site (n = 668). For the
donor it is the U1 duplex energy, exhaustive over all 262,144 9-mers (n = 115);
GT was not pinned there and appears anyway.

Figure X. Sequence determinants learned by the CNN, and the effect of implanting a
splice site into a competitor. Reported model on its held-out split: 14,198 peaks in
1,623 genes (1,623 real 3' ends, 12,575 competitors), intact within-gene win-rate
0.821. (A) Left, logo of each of the 32 first-layer filters (sites above 30% of the
filter's maximum), labelled with information content per column and activating-site
count. Right, the signed contribution of that filter in each of 25 bands of 100 nt,
measured by silencing one filter in one band at a time: the mean intact-minus-
occluded logit at real 3' ends minus the same at competitors. Blue, the filter
argues for the real end in that band; orange, against. Rows are ordered by total
absolute contribution and each logo is paired to its row by an in-code consensus
check. Contribution concentrates in the few hundred nucleotides upstream of the
cleavage site, and the largest detector is a polypyrimidine filter (filter 27, 0.82
bits per column, 24,668 sites). (B) Each construct is implanted at -230 in tandem
copies into every held-out competitor window and rescored. Points, median predicted
probability over 12,575 windows with 95% bootstrap intervals (2,000 resamples);
dashed line, untouched competitors (0.63); grey band, the envelope of 20
mononucleotide shuffles and all 9 derangements. A 3' splice site
(TTTAACTTTCTTTTTTACAGACG, 83% pyrimidine, no G, score3 10.28) lifts the median to
0.799 (0.792-0.805) at one copy and 0.985 at four; a 5' splice site (CAGGTGAGT, the
strongest U1 duplex, score5 10.86) gives 0.593 (0.584-0.604) at one copy and 0.331
at eight. The logos beside each part are ensembles sampled near the optimum of the
model (acceptor, n = 668, AG pinned, score3 >= 10) and of the U1 duplex (donor,
n = 115, exhaustive over 9-mers, GT not pinned).

Parameters

  B  implant position -230; 12,575 competitor windows; 2,000 bootstrap resamples;
     20 mononucleotide shuffles; 9 derangements

     score3 floor 10.0, tract >= 80% pyrimidine, no G, 668 sequences kept

Constructs tested but deliberately NOT drawn, worth a sentence in the text
  acc_s3max (TTTCTTCTCTCTCCCTGCAGGTG, score3 forced above 16, near the MaxEntScan
    optimum) moves the median by at most +0.02 and falls below baseline at 4 copies:
    the better the acceptor by MaxEnt, the less this model wants it.
  acc_best (the MaxEntScan optimum itself) is negative at every dose.
  acc_noC (the T-rich unit) is excluded because its own shuffle scores HIGHER than
    the intact sequence, so the effect there is composition, not order; that is why
    the controls are drawn rather than subtracted throughout.
  acc_model (61% pyrimidine, 28% G) reaches 0.916 at one copy; acc_canon is drawn
    instead because it answers the objection that acc_model does not look like a
    3' splice site, and the preference survives the constraint.
