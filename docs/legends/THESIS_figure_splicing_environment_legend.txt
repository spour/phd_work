Figure X. Real 3' ends occupy splice-donor-depleted sequence relative to
intragenic competitors, on two redundant measures of the same donor axis.
All panels derive from a single scan of one transcript-oriented window,
-2,000 to +500 nt about the peak centre (2,501 offsets per site), over 4,847 real
3' ends, 43,547 intragenic competitors and 30,000 matched intronic decoys drawn
from 4,847 genes (one real 3' end and at least one competitor per gene). Offset 0
is the poly(A) site; negative offsets are upstream along the transcript.
(A) Metagene density of strong 5' splice sites (MaxEntScan score5 >= 8) in 100 nt
bins, expressed per kb of scanned sequence, for real 3' ends (blue), competitors
(orange) and intronic decoys (purple). Lines are unsmoothed bin means; shaded
ribbons are +/- 1 SEM across sites within a class; the dashed line marks the
poly(A) site and the shaded field the upstream side. Donor density is depleted at
real 3' ends and the depletion is confined upstream: in the bin immediately
upstream (-50 nt, the minimum of the real-end profile) the density is 0.54 +/- 0.033
per kb at real ends versus 0.76 +/- 0.013 at competitors and 0.87 +/- 0.017 at decoys
(mean over the full 500 nt upstream, 0.64, 0.81 and 0.87), whereas over +1 to +500
nt the three classes converge to 0.81, 0.83 and 0.82 per kb. Decoys are intronic
and matched to competitors on distance to the canonical poly(A) site and on
splice-site context, so the depletion tracks the 3' end rather than intronic
location. (B) Within-gene contrast on the same window, reducing each site to the
number of U1 sites (duplex -dG >= 4 kcal/mol) per kb and each gene to the value at
its real 3' end minus the mean over its own competitors. Histogram and kernel
density of the 4,847 per-gene differences; the dashed line marks no difference and
the solid blue line the mean. Real ends carry 10.9 fewer U1 sites per kb (median
12.2 fewer; 66.7% of genes below zero; two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank on the paired
differences, V = 3,386,212, n = 4,836 after excluding 11 genes with a difference of
exactly zero, z = -25.35, P < 2.2 x 10^-16). The distribution is unimodal and
straddles zero, so this is a shift in a population-level property and not a
per-site discriminator. (C) Joint distribution of the two donor scores over every
9-mer position of every window. Main panel, the 10,723,361 GT-anchored positions
(9-mer with G and T at positions 4 and 5, the MaxEntScan donor anchor), binned at
0.5 kcal/mol x 1 bit with a log10 colour scale; the two scores correlate at Pearson
r = 0.692, and equally within each class (0.685, 0.691 and 0.694 for real ends,
competitors and decoys). Inset, all 196,063,340 scanned positions, r = 0.356: the
correlation is diluted, not contradicted, because only 5.47% of positions carry a
GT and at the remainder no donor exists for either score to measure. The two
measurements are therefore treated throughout as one donor axis, and their
agreement is not counted as independent evidence. (D) Empirical exceedance,
P(score >= t), pooled over all classes and plotted on a log10 ordinate for U1
duplex free energy (left) and the MaxEntScan 5' splice-site score (right), with the
three cuts used in this work marked (orange): -dG >= 4 admits 9.4% of scanned
positions, -dG >= 7 admits 0.59% and score5 >= 8 admits 0.084% (20.57%, 2.77% and
1.54% of GT-anchored positions). Each cut lies inside a smoothly decaying tail
rather than at a break in the distribution: across the two score units either side
the exceedance falls by a steady 2.1-, 2.4- and 2.0-fold per unit. Histogram edges
are placed so that 4, 7 and 8 fall exactly on an edge, making P(score >= t) exact
at each threshold. The thresholds are conventions on a continuum.
U1 duplex free energy is the ViennaRNA duplexfold energy between each 9-mer read as
RNA and the U1 snRNA 5' end 5'-ACUUACCUG-3', sign-inverted and reported as -dG in
kcal/mol, so that larger values indicate stronger predicted U1 binding. No panel
reports a model, a classifier or a ranking metric.

Figure X. Real 3' ends occupy splice-donor-depleted sequence relative to
intragenic competitors. All panels come from one scan of the window -2,000 to +500
nt about the peak centre, over 4,847 real 3' ends, 43,547 competitors and 30,000
matched intronic decoys in 4,847 genes; offset 0 is the poly(A) site.
(A) Density of strong 5' splice sites (MaxEntScan score5 >= 8) per kb in 100 nt
bins; lines are unsmoothed bin means, ribbons +/- 1 SEM across sites. Depletion at
real 3' ends is confined upstream: 0.54 versus 0.76 and 0.87 per kb in the -50 nt

intronic and matched to competitors on distance to the canonical poly(A) site and

and kernel density, n = 4,847 genes; dashed line, no difference; blue line, mean).
Mean -10.9 and median -12.2 per kb, 66.7% of genes below zero; two-sided Wilcoxon
signed-rank V = 3,386,212, n = 4,836 (11 zero differences excluded), z = -25.35,
P < 2.2 x 10^-16. (C) Joint density of U1 duplex free energy and MaxEntScan
score5 over the 10,723,361 GT-anchored positions (0.5 kcal/mol x 1 bit bins,
log10 colour): Pearson r = 0.692, and 0.685, 0.691, 0.694 within the three classes.
Inset, all 196,063,340 scanned positions, r = 0.356, diluted because only 5.47%
carry a GT. The two scores are one donor axis. (D) Exceedance P(score >= t),
pooled, log10 ordinate, for each score; marked cuts (orange) admit 9.4%
(-dG >= 4), 0.59% (-dG >= 7) and 0.084% (score5 >= 8) of scanned positions and lie
in smoothly decaying tails (2.1-, 2.4- and 2.0-fold per unit score across the two
units either side), so they are conventions on a continuum rather than breaks in
the distribution.

Points a referee is likely to raise, already answered in the tree
  - Panel A is deliberately unsmoothed: a 3-bin rolling mean lifts the -50 nt
    minimum from 0.54 to 0.61, so the quoted value would no longer lie on the
    plotted curve. The SEM ribbons carry the noise.
  - Scoring every 9-mer rather than only GT-anchored ones does not affect any
    strong-donor count: no non-GT 9-mer in the 4^9 space exceeds 4.16 bits, so the
    set of positions at score5 >= 8 is identical either way.
  - The U1 score is composition-dominated: in u1_threshold_calibrate.py no
    threshold in [3, 9] reaches an FDR of 0.10 against a dinucleotide-shuffle null
    (minimum 0.84 at -dG = 7). A U1 site here is a position of favourable
    composition for U1 hybridization, not a detected motif; no motif claim is made.
  - The measured 9.40% exceedance at -dG >= 4 agrees to 0.10 percentage points with
    the 9.30% that calibration found independently on its own peak windows.
